CONSUMER RIGHTS

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Consumer Rights include:

1. Right to Safety
2. Right to Information
3. Right to Choose
4. Right to be Heard
5. Right to Seek Redressal
6. Right to Consumer Education

If a consumer receives defective products or poor services, they may file a complaint before the Consumer Commission.

Consumer disputes can also be filed online through the National Consumer Helpline portal.
