LEGAL TERMS

FIR
First Information Report.

Bail
Temporary release of an accused person under legal conditions.

Affidavit
A written statement confirmed by oath.

Petitioner
The person who files a legal petition.

Respondent
The party against whom the petition is filed.

Cognizable Offence
Police may register an FIR and investigate without prior court permission.

Non-Cognizable Offence
Police generally require court permission before investigation.
